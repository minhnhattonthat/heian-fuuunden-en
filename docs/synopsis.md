# Synopsis - Heian Fuuunden (who is who; grows as batches are translated)

- Setting: Heian Kyoto and the provinces, 60 years after Taira no Masakado's revolt (late 10th century).
- Opening (ids 131-13B): Kamo no Hiroyuki visits Abe no Seimei. The enemy means to erase Kyoto with the Yamawaro's
  great art, the Rite of Transference. Seimei adds that this year suits Raising the Dead (Masakado's 60-year cycle);
  only Seimei and his former disciple Doma (missing 15 years) know that art. Seimei sends to Priest Tenkai at Kumano
  for the help of Kagura, a boy Tenkai named and raised, whose fate Seimei seems to know.
- Prologue (ids 290-296, palace scene 297-2A4): Emperor Kazan's consort Lady Kishi (daughter of Fujiwara no
  Tamemitsu) dies in 985; after her death twin boys are born. A child born of a dead mother is ill-omened, so Seimei
  takes the living twin to an acquaintance at Kumano (Tenkai); the other twin, said to be dead, is given to Seimei's
  disciple Ashiya Doman to bury. In 1000 (Choho 2) Kagura, a boy of 16 in Kumano, is that twin. Don't state that
  Kagura is the Emperor's son before the script does.
- Kagura: the hero (he/him) and main unit; leads the Kagura Squad. Other squads: Seimei, Hiroyuki, Moritaka, Jogu,
  Momiji.
- Battle death lines (1D4-202) are each ally's last words; 1D4 is likely Tenkai ("I have lived to eighty").
- Allies are recruited province by province (party names 054-083); many endings (epilogues per ally group).
- Key items: Hetsu Mirror (map), Tatemuki Board (entry to Iyo), Hangon Jewel (breaks Masakado's barrier). Shikoku is
  sealed by a triple barrier held by the brothers Akana, Midorina and Aona (Akana's wife Usagino).
- Foes: Doma (and a False Doma), Taira no Masakado, Fujiwara no Sumitomo, the Yamawaro and their captains, bandits,
  oni and tengu. Retired Emperor Kazan and the Kebiishi appear in the capital.
